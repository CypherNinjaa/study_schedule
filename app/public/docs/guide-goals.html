Goals

Bigger targets that span weeks or months — "study 100 hours this semester," "finish the syllabus," "maintain a 14-day study streak." Goals track their own progress, some of them automatically.

What a goal contains

FieldMeaning
Title & descriptionWhat you're aiming for and why.
Target & unitThe number to reach and its unit (hours, sessions, tasks, days…).
CurrentProgress so far — updated automatically or by you.
DeadlineWhen it should be done by.
Category & priorityFor grouping and focus.
StatusActive, completed, or failed.

Automatic vs manual goals

Goals come in two flavours:

  • Automatic — the app measures progress for you from your data. A "study 100 hours" goal fills as you log study sessions; a streak goal tracks your live streak. You never update it by hand.
  • Manual — you set the current value yourself, for things the app can't measure (like "read 5 chapters").

Automatic goals are recomputed every time your data changes, so their progress bars are always current without any bookkeeping from you.

The goal lifecycle

  1. Active

    In progress, with a live progress bar toward the target.

  2. Completed

    You reached the target. This awards +75 XP (and weekly goals award +100).

  3. Failed

    The deadline passed without reaching the target. Automatic goals mark themselves failed so your goal list stays honest — a clear signal to reset or rescope.

Creating a good goal

  1. Make it measurable

    Pick a target and unit the app (or you) can actually track — "50 coding hours," not "get better at coding."

  2. Give it a real deadline

    A deadline turns a wish into a plan and lets the app tell you if you're on pace.

  3. Prefer automatic where possible

    If the app can measure it from study, coding, tasks, or streaks, let it — less manual upkeep, more accuracy.

Tie goals to exams

Around exam season, set a study-hours goal per subject with the exam as the deadline. Combined with the exam-proximity insights and subject progress, you'll always know which subject needs attention next.
