Streaks & freezes

Streaks reward showing up day after day. The app tracks six of them, each with its own rule for what "counting" means — plus a streak freeze so one off day doesn't erase weeks of consistency.

The six streaks

Each streak counts consecutive days on which you met its condition. Miss a day and it resets; meet it and it grows.

StreakQualifies when, on a given day, you…
🔥 Studyreach your daily study-minutes target
💻 Codingreach your daily coding-minutes target
🏃 Exerciselog at least some exercise for the day
🌅 Early wakewake at or before your wake-time target (with a small grace window)
✅ Taskscomplete all your planned tasks for the day
🔁 Habitscomplete all habits that were due that day

The early-wake streak includes a 15-minute grace — waking a few minutes late still counts, so the streak reflects your real routine rather than punishing you to the second.

How streaks are counted

Streaks are always derived from your history, not stored as a fragile counter. That means they stay correct even if you edit a past day or re-import your data — the app recomputes them from the underlying records.

Two important behaviours:

  • Today never breaks a streak. A streak only resets once a qualifying day has fully passed without the condition met. So at 2pm, a study streak you haven't earned yet today is still shown as alive — you have until the day ends.
  • Best streak is remembered. Alongside the current streak, the app keeps your all-time best for each type, so a reset never erases the memory of your record.

Streak freezes

Life happens. A streak freeze protects a streak on a day you couldn't meet the condition, so a single miss doesn't wipe out weeks of momentum.

  • You get one free freeze per week by default (adjustable in Settings).
  • Applying a freeze to a day keeps the streak intact across that gap.
  • You can remove a freeze if you applied it by mistake.
  • Unused freezes don't stockpile indefinitely — the weekly allowance keeps them meaningful.
Use freezes deliberately

A freeze is best spent on a genuine exception — a travel day, an exam, illness — not as a routine crutch. The app will even nudge you when a long streak is at risk so you can decide: push through today, or spend a freeze.

Where streaks appear

  • Dashboard — your active streaks front and centre.
  • Insights — timely warnings like "Your 8-day study streak needs today."
  • Analytics — streak history over time.
  • Achievements — many badges are tied to streak milestones (3, 7, 30 days, and beyond).
