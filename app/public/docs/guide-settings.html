Your data controls

This is the heart of the local-first promise. You own the file, and these tools make that real:

ActionWhat it does
Export / backupSave a copy of your entire data file whenever you want — before a big change, or just for safekeeping.
ImportLoad a previously exported file, e.g. to restore or move to another device.
Reconnect fileRe-link your data file if the browser lost access (see Storage).
Storage modeSee whether you're using a real file or the browser mirror, and switch if your browser supports it.
Destructive actions always confirm

Anything that clears or overwrites data asks you to confirm first, and the app never silently discards your information. If it ever finds your file is corrupt, it refuses to overwrite it — protecting whatever might be recoverable. Read Privacy & data safety before using reset tools.

Export before anything big

A ten-second export before switching devices, clearing browser data, or trying an import gives you a safety net. Keep the odd backup in your own cloud folder and you can never truly lose your progress.
