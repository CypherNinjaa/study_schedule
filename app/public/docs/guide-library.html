Library & Ebook Reader

Save, organize, and read your textbooks, research papers, and ebooks locally with an interactive 3D page flip reader and eye-comfort settings.

Private, local-first reading

The Library module is a built-in ebook reader and bookshelf designed specifically for focused studying. Your books, PDFs, reading progress, and covers are stored entirely on your computer inside IndexedDB — nothing is uploaded to the cloud.

Library view modes

You can switch between three distinct view modes using the toolbar toggles:

  • 3D Bookshelf View: An interactive 3D virtual library with realistic wooden shelves, custom book covers, dynamic spine thicknesses based on page count, and reading progress bars on spines.
  • Grid View: Clean 2:3 aspect ratio cards displaying book cover thumbnails, linked subject badges, progress bars, and quick actions.
  • Compact List View: Space-efficient rows ideal for large textbook collections, showing file sizes, formats, and last opened timestamps.

3D page flip engine

When reading in 3D Book Flip mode, the reader simulates an authentic open book:

  1. Bidirectional flipping

    Click on the right half of the book or press → / Space to flip forward (Right-to-Left). Click on the left half or press ← to flip backward (Left-to-Right).

  2. Responsive window auto-fit

    The reader automatically detects your PDF aspect ratio and window dimensions, scaling the 2-page book spread to fill your screen with zero text cutoff.

  3. Realistic paper lighting

    Pages rotate in true 3D perspective with dynamic shadow sweeps and central spine crease shading.

Productivity & analytics integration

Reading is treated as an active study activity. When you finish a reading session in the reader:

  • Study sessions: Time spent reading automatically logs as a study session with method reading.
  • Daily targets & streaks: Reading minutes count toward your daily study target and maintain your study streak.
  • Subject tracking: If the book is linked to a subject, the time is attributed directly to that subject's syllabus analytics.
  • XP rewards: You earn experience points for every minute of focused reading.
