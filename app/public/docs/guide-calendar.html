Calendar

The bird's-eye view of your commitments across days, weeks, and months — tasks, classes, exams, and deadlines all in one place, so nothing sneaks up on you.

Month, week, and day views

Switch between views depending on how far ahead you're looking:

Month
The big picture — spot busy stretches and free weekends at a glance.
Week
Your seven days with times, useful for balancing study across the week.
Day
A single day in detail; for hour-by-hour planning, the Today planner is the richer tool.

What appears on the calendar

  • Tasks with a date (and time, if set) from your task list.
  • Classes from your recurring timetable.
  • Exam dates from your subjects.
  • Assignment deadlines and other dated items.

Everything is colour-cued by category so the shape of your week is readable in a second.

Working from the calendar

  1. Click a day

    See everything scheduled for it, and add a task directly to that date.

  2. Spot the crunch

    Clusters of deadlines or exams stand out — plan study earlier so you're not cramming.

  3. Jump to detail

    Open any item to edit it, or move into the Today planner for that date.

Scan ahead every Sunday

A one-minute look at the week ahead in month view is the cheapest planning you'll ever do. Pair it with the weekly review workflow to set the week up well.
