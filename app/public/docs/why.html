Why it exists

Productivity OS was built to solve one problem: staying consistent with study and self-improvement, without handing your private life to a company's servers.

The problem it solves

If you're a student trying to balance classes, self-study, coding practice, exercise, sleep, and a personal life, the usual tools fall short in one of two ways:

  • Spreadsheets can record anything, but they don't plan your day, don't motivate you, and turn into abandoned rows within a week.
  • Cloud apps look nice, but they require accounts, push notifications you didn't ask for, and quietly store deeply personal data — how much you sleep, who you spend time with, when you're distracted — on someone else's computer.

This app is the middle path: as capable as a good cloud product, as private as a file on your desk.

The principles behind it

Local-first, always

Your data is stored as a single JSON file on your own device. The app works fully offline. There is no server to sign in to, no database in the cloud, and no analytics watching what you click. If you delete the file, the data is gone — because it was only ever yours. Read more in Privacy & data safety.

Never lose data

Every meaningful change saves automatically. If a file becomes unreadable, the app refuses to overwrite it and keeps a backup instead. You're never one misclick away from losing months of records. See Storage & autosave.

Motivation that respects effort

Gamification only works if it tracks real progress. XP is awarded for genuine actions — finishing a task, completing a study session, keeping a habit — and the system actively prevents "farming" points by toggling the same task on and off. Streaks reward showing up. Levels grow on a curve that stays meaningful. See XP & levels and Streaks & freezes.

Honest numbers

Every statistic, chart, and insight is calculated directly from data you entered. The productivity score shows its full breakdown so you always know why it's 82 and not 90. Insights are plain arithmetic over your history — never guesses, never health advice, never a black box.

Designed for one person

This is a personal tool, tuned for a student's real week — a class timetable, study and coding targets, habits, and a private space for personal time. It's meant to feel like your operating system for getting things done, not a generic dashboard.
