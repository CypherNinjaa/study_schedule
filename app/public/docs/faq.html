Frequently asked questions

Quick answers to the things people wonder about most. If something here doesn't solve it, the troubleshooting guide goes deeper.

Getting started & basics

Is this free? Do I need an account?

Yes, it's free, and there's no account. There's no sign-up, no login, and no server — you just open the app and start. Everything lives on your own device.

Does it work offline?

Completely. The app runs entirely in your browser with no network calls, so it works on a plane, in a library basement, anywhere. There's nothing to be "online" with.

Which browser should I use?

Chrome or Edge are recommended, because they let the app save directly to a real file you control. It still works in other browsers using a built-in browser-storage fallback — see Storage & autosave for the difference.

Do I have to fill in everything to start?

No. The setup wizard gets you going in a few minutes, and you can skip the parts you don't need yet. Add subjects, habits, and your timetable whenever you're ready.

Privacy & data

Where is my data stored? Can anyone see it?

On your device only — in a file you choose, or your browser's local storage. Nothing is uploaded, there's no tracking, and no one else can see it. Read the full promise on Privacy & data safety.

How do I back up or move my data to another device?

Use Export in Settings to save a copy of your data file, then Import it on the other device. That's also your backup — keep an exported copy somewhere safe.

Will I lose my data if I clear my browser or update it?

If you use a real file (Chrome/Edge), your data lives in that file and survives browser changes. If you're on the browser-storage fallback, clearing browser data can remove it — so export a backup first. Either way, an occasional export is your safety net.

Can I use it on my phone?

The interface is fully responsive and works on a phone, with a bottom navigation bar. For heavy data entry and the file-based storage, a laptop or desktop in Chrome/Edge is the smoother experience.

XP, streaks & scoring

Can I cheat to get more XP?

Not really — and that's by design. XP is granted through an idempotent ledger, so toggling a task or habit on and off never pays out twice. Rewards track real actions, which is what makes them worth anything. See XP & levels.

My streak broke even though I studied — why?

Streaks are based on whether the day's condition was met (for example, hitting your study target, or waking within the early-wake grace window). Check that you logged enough to meet the target, and remember today's streak never counts as broken until the day is actually over. Details on Streaks & freezes.

What's a streak freeze and how do I get one?

A freeze protects a streak on a day you can't keep it. You get one free freeze per week by default — use it on a genuine off day rather than losing a long run.

How is my productivity score calculated?

It's a weighted blend of study, tasks, habits, coding, sleep, exercise, and schedule adherence, minus a distraction penalty. The full weights and labels are on Productivity score.

Features & usage

What's the difference between a task and a habit?

A task is one-off — do it and it's done. A habit repeats on a schedule and builds a streak; completing it today just means it comes back on its next due day.

What's the difference between the Calendar, Timetable, and Today planner?

The Timetable is your repeating weekly class schedule. The Calendar shows specific dated items across weeks and months. The Today planner is the detailed hour-by-hour view of a single day.

Do I have to use the Pomodoro timer to log study?

No. You can enter a study session manually with its duration, or let a completed Pomodoro log it automatically. Both count the same way.

Can I change the seeded subjects and timetable?

Absolutely — everything seeded is fully editable. Rename subjects, adjust topics and targets, and reshape the timetable to match your actual semester.
