Timetable

Your recurring weekly class schedule — the fixed skeleton your study time fits around. Set it once and the app knows when you're in class, when you're free, and what's coming up next.

What the timetable is for

Unlike tasks (which are one-off) or the calendar (which shows specific dates), the timetable is your repeating week: the lectures, labs, and tutorials that happen on the same days every week. Once it's in, the app can reason about your real availability instead of assuming every hour is free.

What each class holds

Subject
Which course the class belongs to.
Day & time
The weekday plus start and end time.
Type
Lecture, lab, tutorial, and so on.
Location
Room or building, so it's on hand when you need it.
Teacher
Optional — who takes the class.

Your seeded week

If you accepted the timetable seed during setup, your real BCA-V (Section A) weekly schedule is already in place, mapped to the six seeded subjects. You can edit any class, add missing ones, or clear it and start fresh — it's yours to adjust as the semester changes.

How the timetable helps everywhere else

  • Today & Dashboard — your classes appear alongside tasks so your day is complete, and a "next class" hint tells you what's coming.
  • Free-time awareness — the planner knows the gaps between classes, so study suggestions land in time you actually have.
  • Realistic planning — you won't over-schedule study into hours you're sitting in a lecture.
Keep it current

When your schedule changes — a rescheduled lab, a dropped elective — update the timetable straight away. Everything that depends on your availability gets more accurate the moment you do.
