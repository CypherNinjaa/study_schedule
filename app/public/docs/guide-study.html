Study & Pomodoro

Log what you actually studied, run focused Pomodoro sessions, and build an honest record of your learning time — by subject, method, and day.

Study sessions

A study session is a record of a real chunk of studying. Each one captures:

Subject & topic
What you studied, linked to your subjects for subject-level stats.
Duration
How many minutes — entered directly or captured by the timer.
Method
How you studied (see below).
Concentration & difficulty
Optional self-ratings to spot which conditions work best for you.
Notes
Anything worth remembering about the session.

Study methods

Tagging the method makes your analytics far richer — you can see whether you spend enough time on active recall versus passive reading. Available methods:

Reading Practice Revision Problem solving Watching lecture Coding Mock test Flashcards

Sessions logged with the Coding method count toward your coding minutes and coding streak, not just study — so coding practice feeds the right targets automatically.

XP from studying

Completing a study session earns XP that scales with length, so longer focused sessions are rewarded — up to a sensible cap that stops one marathon from dominating:

study XP = 10 + 5 per 30 minutes   (maximum 40)

Hitting your full daily study target adds a further +50 bonus. See XP & levels.

The Pomodoro timer

Built in for focused work. The classic technique alternates a focus block with a short break; after several rounds you take a longer break. The default is 25 minutes focus / 5 minutes break, and you can customise it:

PresetFocusBreak
Short15 min5 min
Classic (default)25 min5 min
Long45 min10 min
Deep50 min10 min
Extended60 min15 min

You can also set how many focus rounds happen before a long break. Adjust all of this in Settings.

What happens when a Pomodoro completes

  1. Audible chime & notification

    A multi-tone ascending alert chime sounds, the browser tab title blinks, and a desktop notification triggers.

  2. A study session is logged

    The exact elapsed wall-clock focus time is recorded automatically against your chosen subject.

  3. XP is awarded & confetti celebrates

    +15 XP for the completed Pomodoro, accompanied by celebration particle animations.

  4. Stats and streaks update

    Study minutes, your study ring, and the study streak all update immediately.

  5. It autosaves

    No manual save needed.

Saving early (Save & Stop)

If your study session gets interrupted or you finish your focus block ahead of schedule, click Save & Stop. The app accurately computes the actual minutes you spent studying up to that moment and records the study session without penalising or discarding your hard work.

What the Study page shows

  • Today's, this week's, and this month's study time.
  • Subject-wise breakdown — where your hours actually went.
  • Study consistency and progress toward your daily target.
  • Today's completed Pomodoros.
Log honestly, even the short ones

Fifteen focused minutes recorded truthfully is worth more to your future self than an exaggerated hour. Consistent, accurate logging is what makes the trends on Analytics genuinely useful.
