Today & planner

The Today page is where you plan and run a single day — a timeline that blends your classes, planned blocks, and tasks into one place to work from.

What the planner shows

Today lays your day out as a timeline, from your wake time to your sleep time. On it you'll see:

  • Classes pulled from your timetable for that weekday.
  • Tasks scheduled for the day, with their times, categories, and status.
  • Routine anchors like wake, travel, and sleep, derived from your settings.

It's the practical counterpart to the dashboard: the dashboard summarises, Today is where you actually do the work.

Planning your day

  1. Review what's fixed

    Your classes and routine anchors are already placed. See the gaps between them — that's your available time.

  2. Add tasks into the gaps

    Create tasks and give them a start time so they land on the timeline. Use the quick-add (the + button or Ctrl + K → "Add task").

  3. Order by priority

    Put your most important work in your best hours. The app highlights high and critical priority tasks.

Working through the day

As you go, keep the timeline honest by updating each item's status. Every task supports these states:

StatusUse it when
To doNot started yet.
In progressYou're actively working on it.
CompletedDone — awards XP and updates streaks and score.
SkippedConsciously not doing it today.
PostponedMoving it to another day.
Skip and postpone are features, not failures

An accurate day is more useful than a perfect-looking one. Marking something skipped or postponed keeps your analytics truthful and prevents overdue clutter. The app even nudges you to clear out stale overdue tasks.

Turning free time into progress

Because the planner knows your fixed commitments, it can point out genuinely open time. When there's a meaningful gap, that's your cue to slot in a study block or a task — a short session still moves a subject forward. See the free-time insight on Analytics.

Recurring items

Things you do every day or every week — a morning revision block, daily coding practice — can be set to recur so you don't recreate them each day. Recurring templates roll forward automatically. Set recurrence when creating a task; see Tasks.
