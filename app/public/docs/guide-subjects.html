Subjects & exams

Manage each course as a living record: its syllabus topics, assignments, exam date, and how much you've actually studied it. This is where study time turns into visible syllabus progress.

What a subject holds

Name & code
e.g. "Time Series Analysis" (STAT311).
Teacher & credits
Who teaches it and its credit weight.
Difficulty & target hours
How hard it is for you and how many hours you're aiming to put in.
Topics
The syllabus broken into modules and topics you can tick off.
Assignments
Coursework with due dates.
Exam date
Powers exam-countdown insights.
Notes
Anything subject-specific worth keeping.

Subject progress

Each subject shows a progress bar built from two things working together:

  • Topics completed — how much of the syllabus you've ticked off.
  • Study time logged — hours recorded against that subject via study sessions and Pomodoros.

Because study sessions can be tagged with a subject, simply studying and logging it keeps these bars moving without extra admin.

Exams and countdowns

Set an exam date and the app watches the calendar for you. As an exam approaches (within about three weeks), an insight appears telling you how many days remain, how much you've studied that subject recently, and how many topics are still open — turning urgency into a clear next action.

Neglected-subject alerts

If a subject gets no study time for two weeks, Analytics flags it. It's an easy way to catch the course you've quietly been avoiding before it becomes a problem.

A simple subject workflow

  1. Keep topics current

    Tick topics as you finish them so progress reflects reality.

  2. Tag your study

    Always pick the subject when logging a session — it's what powers subject analytics.

  3. Set exam dates early

    The countdown insights only help if the dates are in.

  4. Review before exams

    Use subject progress plus goals to decide where the next hour should go.
