Achievements & rewards

The motivation layer. Levels, badges, and milestones turn steady effort into visible progress — designed to reward real work, never gaming the system.

Levels and XP

Everything you do that counts — completing tasks, logging study, keeping habits, finishing goals — earns XP. XP accumulates into levels along a curve that gets steeper as you climb, so early levels come quickly and later ones feel earned:

Your current level, XP, and progress to the next level appear on the dashboard and in the top bar.

Badges and milestones

Badges recognise specific accomplishments — the kind of moments worth marking:

  • Streak milestones — reaching 7, 30, or 100 days on a streak.
  • Volume milestones — total study hours, tasks completed, Pomodoros run.
  • Consistency — habit streaks and reliable daily activity.
  • Goals — completing goals, especially ambitious ones.

Locked badges show what's still ahead, so there's always a next thing to aim for.

Rewards are calculated from the same anti-farming ledger as XP. You can't unlock a badge by toggling something on and off — it has to be genuinely earned. See how XP stays honest.

Streaks on display

Your six streaks — study, coding, exercise, early wake, tasks, and habits — are shown here with their current and best values, plus your one weekly streak freeze if you need to protect a run on an off day.

Why it's built this way

Gamification here exists to make honest effort feel rewarding — not to trick you into busywork. Because every reward traces back to real, verified actions, the numbers stay meaningful. A high level means you actually did the work.

Chase the habit, not the badge

Badges are a nice side effect of good routines. Aim for the routine — consistent study and kept habits — and the achievements take care of themselves.
