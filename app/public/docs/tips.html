Tips & best practices

Small habits that make the difference between an app you abandon in a week and one that quietly changes how you study. Most of these take seconds; all of them compound.

Log honestly, log lightly

  • Record the short sessions too — fifteen honest minutes beats an exaggerated hour for your future self.
  • Don't inflate priority and difficulty. Marking everything "critical / 5" just makes your numbers meaningless.
  • Tag the subject and method every time you study — it's what powers the best insights.
  • If you forget to log something, add it later. Approximately right is far better than nothing.

Aim for consistency over intensity

  • A daily 45 minutes beats a once-a-week marathon — and your streaks reward exactly that.
  • Keep habits few. Three to five you actually keep beats fifteen you ignore.
  • Use a freeze on a genuine off day instead of breaking a long run — that's what it's for.
  • Let the Pomodoro timer carry you through resistance. Starting one round is the hardest part.

Plan a little, adjust often

  • Two minutes each morning on the Today planner beats an elaborate plan you never follow.
  • Set exam dates the moment you know them so countdown insights have time to help.
  • Prefer automatic goals — let the app measure progress instead of you updating numbers.
  • Review weekly and change one thing. One deliberate adjustment per week adds up fast.

Protect your data

  • Use Chrome or Edge so your data saves to a real file you control (why it matters).
  • Export a backup weekly, and always before switching devices or clearing browser data.
  • Keep a backup copy in your own cloud folder — then you can never truly lose progress.
  • Watch the save indicator. If it says "Disconnected," reconnect your file before doing more work.

Work faster

  • Learn Ctrl + K. The command palette is faster than clicking for almost everything.
  • Use quick-add for tasks — title, priority, date, done. Fill in the rest later only if it helps.
  • Set up recurring tasks for anything you repeat so you never recreate them.
  • Match the theme to your surroundings — dark for night study reduces eye strain.
The one rule that matters most

Be honest with the app and it will be honest with you. Every number here is only as trustworthy as the effort you actually put in — which is exactly what makes an accurate high score feel so good.
