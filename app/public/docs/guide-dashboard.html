Dashboard

Your daily home base. Open the app and the dashboard tells you, in one glance, how today is going and what to do next.

What's on the dashboard

The dashboard gathers the most important signals from every other part of the app into a single screen:

Greeting
A time-aware hello using your name — "Good morning, Vikash 👋".
Level & XP
Your current level, a progress bar toward the next, and today's XP.
Streaks
Your active streaks (study, coding, and more) with their current counts.
Progress rings
Today's completion for Study, Tasks, Habits, and Coding against your targets.
Productivity score
Today's 0–100 score with its label, and the factor breakdown.
Today's schedule
Your timeline for the day — classes, planned blocks, and tasks.
Next task
The single most relevant thing to do now, with quick actions.
Insights
A few timely, data-driven observations (see Analytics).

Reading the progress rings

Each ring fills as you approach that target for the day. They map directly to your daily goals set during onboarding (and changeable in Settings):

  • Study — minutes studied vs your study target.
  • Tasks — tasks completed vs planned today.
  • Habits — habits done vs due today.
  • Coding — coding minutes vs your coding target.

These same four factors are the biggest contributors to your productivity score, so filling the rings and raising the score go hand in hand.

The "next task" card

Rather than making you scan a list, the dashboard surfaces the next thing worth doing and gives you one-tap actions:

  • Start — begin working on it (and, where relevant, start a timer).
  • Complete — mark it done, which awards XP and updates your streaks and score instantly.
Watch it update live

Complete a task from here and you'll see the rings move, XP float up, and the score recalculate immediately — no refresh, no save button. That's the whole app working as one system.

A good dashboard habit

Open the dashboard first thing each morning to see your plan, and again in the evening to check your rings before the day closes. For a full routine, see Everyday workflows.
