Quick start

From opening the app to landing on your dashboard takes about three minutes. Here's exactly what happens the first time you run Productivity OS.

Before you begin

The app runs in your web browser. For the best experience — saving directly to a file on your computer — use Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge on a desktop or laptop. Other browsers (including Firefox, Safari, and most mobile browsers) work too, but they save inside the browser instead of to a file you pick. See Storage & autosave for the difference.

Nothing you enter during setup is sent anywhere. Your name, times, and targets stay on your device from the very first keystroke.

The first-run setup

When you open the app for the first time, a welcome wizard walks you through eight quick steps. A progress bar at the top shows where you are, and you can move Back and Next freely.

  1. Welcome

    A short introduction to what the app does. Nothing to fill in — just read and click Next.

  2. Choose where your data lives

    This is the most important step. You have three choices:

    • Create a data file — pick a location like Documents/Study Productivity/productivity-data.json. Everything autosaves there from now on. (Chrome/Edge only.)
    • Open an existing data file — continue from a file you set up before, perhaps on another device.
    • Just use this browser for now — start immediately; your data is kept safely inside the browser. You can connect a file later from Settings.

  3. Your name

    Used only for greetings like "Good morning, Vikash." It never leaves your device. Leave it blank to use the default.

  4. Your daily rhythm

    Set your usual wake and sleep times. The app shows how much sleep that implies (for example, "about 5h 0m"). These are your own targets — adjustable any time — not medical advice.

  5. Daily targets

    Use the sliders to set daily goals for study, coding, and exercise. These drive your progress rings and productivity score. Sensible defaults are pre-filled (study 3h, coding 1h30m, exercise 30m).

  6. Your subjects

    Optionally load a full set of BCA-V subjects, each pre-filled with its syllabus modules. Everything stays editable, and you can skip this if you'd rather start empty.

  7. Your class timetable

    Optionally load the Monday–Friday BCA-V (A) schedule, complete with teachers, rooms, and lab sessions. Every class can be edited, moved, or removed later on the Timetable page.

  8. Pick your starting habits

    Choose a handful of habits to begin with from the seeded list (Study, Wake up early, Exercise, Read, Code, and more). Keep it small — you can always add more. Click Start to finish.

The moment you click Start, your setup is saved and you land on the Dashboard. That's it — you're running.

What to do next

Learn the interface

Find your way around the sidebar, top bar, and save indicator.

Try a daily workflow

Plan your morning, run a study block, and review your day.

Add your first task

Press Ctrl+K or the + button and create something to do today.
Tip: connect a file even if you skipped it

If you chose "just use this browser," open Settings → Data whenever you're ready and connect a real file. Your existing data moves across automatically.
