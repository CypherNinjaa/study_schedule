Tasks

A full task manager for everything you need to get done — study, college work, coding, projects, and personal to-dos — with priorities, categories, difficulty, and recurrence.

Anatomy of a task

Each task can carry as much or as little detail as you like. The full set of fields:

FieldWhat it's for
TitleA short description of what to do.
Description & notesExtra detail, links, or context.
DateThe day it belongs to.
Start & end timeOptional — placing it on the Today timeline and calendar.
CategoryStudy, College, Coding, Project, Assignment, Exercise, Personal, Relationship, or Other.
SubjectOptionally link it to a subject for subject-level analytics.
PriorityLow, Medium, High, or Critical.
Difficulty1–5 — feeds the XP reward.
Estimated / actual durationPlan vs reality, useful for improving your estimates.
StatusTo do, In progress, Completed, Skipped, or Postponed.
RecurringRepeat daily, weekly, or on a custom pattern.

Creating a task

The fastest way is quick-add: press Ctrl + K and choose "Add task," or tap the + button. Type a title, set a priority and date, and you're done — everything else is optional and can be filled in later.

For detailed tasks, open the task and add its subject, difficulty, times, and notes.

Priority and difficulty

These two fields do more than sort your list — they shape your rewards. Completing a harder, higher-priority task earns more XP:

task XP = 10 + (difficulty − 1) × 4 + priority bonus
priority bonus:  low +0   medium +2   high +5   critical +8

Set them honestly. Marking everything "critical, difficulty 5" just inflates numbers; accurate values make your analytics and XP meaningful. Full details in XP & levels.

Completing tasks

Mark a task complete and a chain of updates happens automatically:

  1. XP is awarded

    Calculated from the task's difficulty and priority — once, thanks to the anti-farming ledger.

  2. Progress updates

    Your Tasks ring and productivity score recalculate on the spot.

  3. Streaks advance

    Completing all planned tasks keeps your task-completion streak alive.

  4. It autosaves

    The change is written to your file within a second.

Un-completing and re-completing a task does not grant XP again. Rewards are tied to real state changes, so your totals stay trustworthy.

Recurring tasks

For anything you repeat — daily revision, weekly assignment review — set it to recur. The app materialises upcoming instances ahead of time so they appear on the right days without you recreating them. Change or stop the recurrence whenever your routine shifts.

Organising and finding tasks

  • Filter by status, category, priority, or subject to focus your view.
  • Search tasks from the command palette (Ctrl + K).
  • Clear overdue items regularly — the app flags tasks left in "to do" past their date so your list stays honest.

To work tasks in the context of a single day, use the Today planner; to see them across weeks, use the Calendar.
