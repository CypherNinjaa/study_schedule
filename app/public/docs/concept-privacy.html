Privacy & data safety

The core promise of Productivity OS: your private productivity data stays on your device, and the app goes to real lengths to make sure you never lose it.

Privacy by design

This isn't a setting you turn on — it's how the app is built. There is simply no code that sends your data anywhere.

  • No servers. Your productivity data is never uploaded. There's no backend to receive it.
  • No accounts. No sign-up, no login, no email. Nothing identifies you.
  • No tracking. No analytics, no telemetry, no cookies following you. Your usage isn't measured.
  • No third parties. Nothing about how you study, sleep, or spend your time is shared with anyone.
  • Works offline. Because it needs no network, you can use it on a plane, and it can't leak what it can't send.

The only time data leaves your device is when you explicitly export or share a file. That's a deliberate action you take, and it's entirely in your control.

Especially the personal stuff

Sensitive metrics like sleep and relationship time are treated as neutral, private numbers. They're shown to you, never judged, and never transmitted.

Never silently lose data

Privacy is only half the promise. The other half is that your months of records don't vanish. Several safeguards work together:

Autosave on every change

There's no "did I save?" moment. Every meaningful edit is written automatically, and pending writes are flushed when you leave the tab. Details in Storage & autosave.

A browser mirror as a safety net

Even in file mode, the app keeps a synced copy inside the browser. If file access drops, saving continues to the mirror so no change is lost while you're disconnected.

Corruption protection

If the app ever opens a file that isn't valid — truncated, hand-edited badly, or damaged by a disk error — it will not overwrite it. Instead it shows a clear message, preserves a backup of the unreadable file, and lets you recover rather than destroying what's there.

"Your data file appears to be corrupted. A backup has been created." — If you see this, don't panic and don't overwrite anything. Head to Troubleshooting for recovery steps.

Confirmation before destruction

The app never deletes your data without asking. Destructive actions — like resetting all data — require an explicit confirmation first. See Settings.

Your part of the deal

Because your data is truly yours, its safety is partly in your hands. A few habits keep it bulletproof:

  • Prefer file mode in Chrome or Edge — a real file survives browser resets.
  • Download a backup weekly, especially if you're in browser mode.
  • Keep backups somewhere separate — a synced folder or external drive.
  • Before clearing browser data, make sure your file is connected or you've exported a copy.
