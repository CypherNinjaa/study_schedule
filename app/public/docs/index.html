The big idea

Most productivity trackers are either a spreadsheet that never motivates you, or a cloud app that quietly harvests your data. Productivity OS is neither. It's a polished daily workspace that combines a planner, a study tracker, a habit tracker, an analytics dashboard, and a gamification system — while keeping every byte of your information on your machine.

  • Local-first. Your data is a JSON file you own. It works offline and never touches a server.
  • Genuinely useful daily. Plan around your real class timetable, then track what actually happened.
  • Motivating, not childish. XP, levels, and streaks reward real effort and resist gaming.
  • Honest analytics. Every number and insight is computed from your own data — nothing is invented.
