XP & levels

Experience points turn real effort into visible progress. Here's exactly how XP is earned, how levels grow, and why you can't cheat the system.

What earns XP

XP is awarded for genuine actions across the app. The main sources:

How task XP scales

A task is worth more when it's harder and more urgent:

task XP = 10 (base)
        + (difficulty − 1) × 4
        + priority bonus

priority bonus:  low +0   medium +2   high +5   critical +8

So a routine task (difficulty 1, low priority) is worth 10 XP, while a hard, critical task (difficulty 5, critical priority) is worth 10 + 16 + 8 = 34 XP. Difficulty is always clamped to the 1–5 range.

How levels work

Your total XP determines your level, and the amount needed for each level grows on a smooth quadratic curve — early levels come quickly to build momentum, later ones take sustained effort.

XP needed to reach level L = 25 × (L−1)² + 75 × (L−1)

Your dashboard shows your current level, a progress bar toward the next one, and how much XP remains. When you cross a threshold, a brief Level up! celebration appears.

Why you can't farm XP

Gamification is worthless if you can game it. The app awards XP based on real state transitions, not repeated clicks. Every award is recorded once against a unique key in a ledger.

Example: Completing a task grants its XP once. If you un-complete it and complete it again, no new XP is granted — the ledger already recorded that task's completion. The same idempotent rule applies to study sessions, Pomodoros, habits, daily goals, and achievements.

This means your level always reflects work you genuinely did. It also means XP totals stay stable and correct even if you edit history or re-import a file.

Where you see XP

  • Dashboard — current level, XP progress bar, and today's XP.
  • Floating "+XP" cues — a subtle animation when you earn points.
  • Analytics — XP progression over time.
  • Daily & weekly reviews — XP earned in the period.
