Exams & Schedule

Schedule mid-terms, final exams, and practicals. Track countdown days, venues, and maintain an interactive syllabus preparation checklist.

Overview

The Exams tab provides a dedicated timeline for your examination milestones. Linking each exam to a semester subject allows you to automatically import syllabus topics and gauge your preparation progress leading up to exam day.

Scheduling an exam

When creating an exam, you can record:

Title
e.g. "End Semester Exam - Core Java" or "Computer Networks Practical".
Subject
Linked subject for automatic code and color tagging.
Date & Time
Day of the exam and reporting/commencement time.
Duration
Exam duration in minutes.
Venue / Hall
Room, hall number, or lab location.
Notes
Admit card requirements, permitted stationery, or calculator allowances.

Syllabus checklist & preparation

Each exam includes an interactive preparation checklist with granular syllabus scope selection:

  • Selective syllabus scope: When linking a subject, you aren't forced to study the entire curriculum. You can select specific modules or individual topics needed for mid-terms, unit tests, or finals.
  • Bulk controls: Use Select all or Clear all shortcuts to quickly configure your exam syllabus scope.
  • Automatic checklist generation: Selected topics are automatically converted into interactive checklist items within the exam card.
  • Custom review items: Add custom revision topics, lab assignments, or previous year question (PYQ) goals directly alongside imported topics.
  • Live preparation bar: Check off topics as you revise to watch your preparation progress bar update in real time.

Dashboard integration

Your upcoming exams automatically display on your Dashboard with color-coded urgency tags (Red for ≤ 3 days, Amber for ≤ 7 days, Accent for longer horizons) so you never get caught off guard by an approaching test.
