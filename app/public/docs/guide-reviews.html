Daily & weekly reviews

A quiet moment to reflect. Reviews turn a day or week of raw activity into a short, honest summary — what you did, how it felt, and what to adjust — so effort compounds into learning.

The daily review

At the end of the day, the app pulls together what actually happened and invites a brief reflection:

  • Your day at a glance — study time, tasks completed, habits kept, and your productivity score.
  • How it felt — a quick mood or energy note, so patterns between feeling and output become visible over time.
  • Wins and blocks — jot what went well and what got in the way.
  • Tomorrow's focus — one or two things to carry forward.

It takes a minute, and completing it keeps your reflection habit alive.

The weekly review

Once a week, a wider lens: totals for the week, streak status, goal progress, subject balance, and the standout insights. It's the natural moment to decide what next week should look like.

  1. Read the week honestly

    Look at the totals and trends without judgement — just notice what's true.

  2. Celebrate what worked

    Name the wins. Consistency deserves acknowledgement.

  3. Pick one adjustment

    Choose a single change for next week — a new habit, a subject to prioritise, a schedule tweak.

  4. Set the week up

    Turn that adjustment into a goal or a few tasks so it actually happens.

Reflection beats more tracking

The tracking is only raw material. The review is where it becomes useful. Five honest minutes reviewing will improve your next week more than an hour of extra logging.

Reviews — including your private notes and mood — are stored in your local file like everything else. They never leave your device. See Privacy & data safety.
