Everyday workflows

The app has a lot of features, but you don't use all of them at once. These are the small, repeatable routines that make it click — copy them, then adapt to your own rhythm.

The two-minute morning plan

Start every day here. It sets your intention and lets the app help you the rest of the day.

  1. Open the Dashboard

    Glance at your streaks, score, and what's already scheduled.

  2. Move to Today

    See your classes from the timetable and any tasks already on the day.

  3. Add what matters

    Drop in the 3–5 things that would make today a win. Set priority honestly so the important ones stand out.

  4. Pick your first focus

    Decide the single thing you'll start with, and start a Pomodoro if it's study.

A focused study session

  1. Choose the subject and method

    Open Study, pick the subject and how you'll study it (reading, practice, problem solving…).

  2. Run a Pomodoro

    Let the timer keep you honest. When it completes, the session logs itself and awards XP.

  3. Tick topics as you go

    Finished a syllabus topic? Mark it complete on the subject so progress reflects reality.

  4. Note anything worth keeping

    A quick note on what was hard helps your future self — and your analytics.

The end-of-day wind-down

  1. Close out tasks

    Mark what you finished complete; skip or postpone what you didn't, so tomorrow starts clean.

  2. Check off habits

    Log the habits you kept — including inverse ones like avoiding social media.

  3. Do the daily review

    Spend a minute on the daily review: how the day felt, one win, one thing for tomorrow.

  4. Let it save

    Everything autosaves — just confirm the "Saved" indicator before you close the tab.

The Sunday weekly review

  1. Read the week

    Open Analytics and the weekly review. Look at trends, not single days.

  2. Scan the calendar ahead

    In the Calendar month view, spot the coming week's deadlines and exams.

  3. Set or adjust a goal

    Pick one thing to improve and turn it into a goal or a few tasks.

  4. Back up

    Export a copy of your data from Settings — a good weekly habit.

Exam-crunch mode

  1. Set the exam date

    On the subject, so countdown insights kick in.

  2. Make a study-hours goal

    Per subject, with the exam as the deadline — an automatic goal that tracks itself.

  3. Let insights steer you

    Follow the "days remaining / topics open" prompts on Analytics to decide where the next hour goes.

  4. Protect your streak if needed

    An intense day of one subject is fine — use a freeze if a streak would otherwise break.

Pick two, not all five

You don't need every workflow at once. Start with the morning plan and the evening wind-down. Those two alone will carry most of the value — add the others when they'd genuinely help.
