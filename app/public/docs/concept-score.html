Productivity score

A single number from 0 to 100 that sums up your day — built from weighted factors you can see, never an arbitrary guess.

The idea

The productivity score answers "how did today go?" at a glance. Crucially, it's fully transparent: the app shows the exact contribution of each factor, so an 82 is always explainable. It rewards balance — a day of nothing but study won't max it out, because tasks, habits, sleep, and the rest all count too.

The factors and their weights

Each factor contributes up to a fixed maximum. You earn a share of that maximum based on how close you got to the relevant target.

FactorMax pointsBased on
Study+30study minutes vs your daily study target
Tasks+25tasks completed vs tasks planned
Habits+15habits done vs habits due today
Coding+10coding minutes vs your daily coding target
Sleep+10how close sleep was to your target
Exercise+5exercise minutes vs your exercise target
Schedule adherence+5time-blocked items you actually completed
Distraction−10 (penalty)social media time over your limit

The positive factors add up to 100. The distraction penalty can subtract up to 10, and the final total is clamped to the 0–100 range.

Example breakdown

A typical strong day might read: Study +30, Tasks +25, Habits +15, Coding +10, Sleep +8, Exercise +5, Adherence +5, Distraction −6 → score 92, "Outstanding". Every line is shown on your dashboard so you know precisely where the points came from.

How each factor is scored

  • Proportional factors (study, tasks, habits, coding, exercise, adherence) give you the fraction of their maximum equal to your progress toward the target. Half your study target ≈ +15 of the 30.
  • Sleep peaks when you're near your target. Both too little and too much pull it down gently, rather than rewarding extremes.
  • Distraction only bites when you exceed your own social-media limit, and it scales with how far over you go — up to the −10 cap.

Factors with no data simply don't count against you — if you had no tasks planned, the tasks factor is treated as neutral rather than a zero.

The score is feedback, not a boss. A sustainable 75 every day beats a burnout 95 followed by three zeros. Use it to spot patterns — which factor keeps costing you points — and adjust. See Analytics for trends over time.
